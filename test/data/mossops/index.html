About Mossop's

Your new gathering place in the heart of Downtown Toronto, steps from the Financial District and Commerce Court. Our menu, inspired by “City Flavours”, is a celebration of simple, humble recipes and local ingredients. Each dish is crafted to honour its’ roots while embracing the spirit of sharing and cultural connection. Delicious coffee from our friendly local roaster Stock & Crown, creative cocktails, and community events sure to hit the spot any time—from dawn until pre-dawn. Whether you are a loyalty member or a curious wanderer, come in for breakfast, lunch, dinner, and everything in-between!

The Mosaic Room

A blank canvas to create a captivating experience no matter what you’re planning – The Mosaic Room will be your next favourite venue. From celebrations and parties to symposiums and pop-up fairs, the opportunities are endless. The best part is you’ll enjoy the Mossop’s Social House menu, inspired by “City Flavours” featuring humble recipes and local ingredients, specialty cocktails, Detour coffee and Metz Luxury Teas.

Interested in hosting your next event at Mossop’s? Get in touch with our team at events@mossops.ca

Social Events & Meetings

Whether you’re planning a team lunch, birthday celebration, networking mixer, or private meeting, The Mosaic Room offers a stylish and flexible space for every occasion. With customizable menus and attentive service, Mossop’s ensures your event is seamless, memorable, and tailored to your needs.
