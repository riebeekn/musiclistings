Max Mr.Monkey

We went on a Saturday night (August 10th, 2024). The servers were super quick and polite. They were able to immediately get to our drinks, which I ordered a beer that was house brewed there. While getting our drinks, we were also able to order our appetizer (pulled pork poutine). Not that much time passed, and they were able to get our poutine while still waiting for drinks. The poutine was to die for. Our drinks came back, and it was one of the best beers I’ve personally had. We were also able to place our main order, which I got the lamb burger. That also didn’t take much time and was really good. They also had a pool table and board games after you eat. I would come back again, and definitely recommend it if you’re in Toronto 🙂
