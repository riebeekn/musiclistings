Common Questions

The piano bar runs Tuesday through Saturday. Tuesday to Thursday the music starts at 7pm and goes until 10:30pm. Friday and Saturday it starts at 8:30pm and runs to midnight. Live piano also plays during Saturday and Sunday brunch from 11:30am to 3pm.

There's no cover charge. Walk in and grab a seat at the bar or reserve a table through the website.

The players cover a wide range — Billy Joel, Elton John, Taylor Swift, Gnarls Barkley, Bohemian Rhapsody. Requests are always welcome.

Walk-ins are welcome. Reserving a table ahead is the safer move on Friday and Saturday nights when it fills up.

The Berczy Tavern is at 69 Front Street East in Old Town Toronto, a few blocks from the St. Lawrence Market.
